💾 LRU (Least Recently Used) Cache

A cache that automatically evicts the least recently used item when full. Perfect for caching query results or frequently accessed data.

How It Works

  • Cache stores up to 5 items
  • When cache is full, the least recently used item is evicted
  • Accessing an item moves it to the front (most recently used)
  • Perfect for caching expensive computations or database queries
