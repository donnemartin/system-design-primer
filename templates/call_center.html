📞 Call Center System

A hierarchical call center that routes incoming calls to available employees. Calls can be escalated from operators to supervisors to directors.

How It Works

  • Calls come in with a desired rank (Operator, Supervisor, Director)
  • System finds next available employee at that level
  • If none available, call is queued
  • Employees can escalate calls to higher levels
